Does hotel californian have a lobby? Yes, hotel californian features a lobby area. Hotel Californian in Santa Barbara features a fully appointed lobby in the original building, which includes 62 guest rooms, a gallery, a gym, and an opulent spa. The second building serves as the main entrance to the hotel and contains 58 guest rooms, a lobby, a restaurant, conference rooms, offices, a lounge, and a library. The Mason Building, part of the hotel complex, includes the hotel's arrival lobby and The Society: State & Mason wine bar, along with 58 guest rooms and five meeting rooms clustered around a picturesque open-air paseo. The State Building houses Santa Barbara's most unique event spaces, including Serena, a 3,500 sq. ft. rooftop event deck atop the 3,000 sq. ft. Alhambra Ballroom, and features the gourmet café Goat Tree, offering casual brunch, gourmet picnic baskets, and to-go options..
